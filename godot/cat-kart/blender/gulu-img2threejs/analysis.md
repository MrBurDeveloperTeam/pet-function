# Gulu reference observation

Reference: ../gulu-clay-v3/reference-sheet.png. User requests the seated kitten silhouette in this clay turnaround, including the tail. Initial deliverable is static editable grey geometry; coat colour, fur strands, animation and game replacement are deferred.

1. Identification: stylized seated juvenile feline, character domain, high confidence. Not a humanoid. Four paws contact the floor in the front and profile views.
2. Form: bilateral organic anatomy with an asymmetric curved tail. Total crown-to-floor height approximately 450 reference pixels; head excluding ears approximately 210 pixels tall and 275 wide. Head is about 0.47 of total height excluding ear tips. Broad cheeks narrow into the chin. Pelvis is wider and deeper than the upper chest; torso leans forward slightly in profile.
3. Hierarchy: continuous cranial/cheek/chin volume with embedded ear roots; shallow eye sockets containing eyeballs, eyelid rims, iris and pupil relief; paired muzzle pads under a small triangular nose, philtrum and mouth grooves. Compact chest joins two tapering forelegs, four rounded toe groups per front paw, seated haunches, rear paws, and a thick curved tail.
4. Connections: ear roots overlap skull; eye rims conform to the face rather than floating circles. Forelegs originate inside chest, without exposed upper caps. Haunches attach to pelvis. Tail begins at rear pelvis and sweeps outward/upward with a round tapered tip.
5. Material: grey matte nonmetallic clay, softer gloss on ocular surfaces. Surface fur impression is not required for silhouette acceptance.
6. Finish: grey-only study. No photographic face projection, stripes or coat colour in this phase.
7. Identity: triangular broad ears, recessed eyes, short muzzle, cheek volume, compact four-paw sitting pose, thick curved tail. Reject protruding spherical eyes, disconnected ears, straight vertical arms, narrow humanoid torso and thin segmented tail.
8. Uncertainty: six generated views are not mutually exact orthographic projections; bottom view rearranges the limbs. Use front and profiles for proportions, back for volume, bottom only for pad placement. No physical scale supplied; normalize height to 1 unit. Target similarity is judged visually, not an exact recovered mesh.

Quality contract: produce a subject-specific procedural Three.js model; measure front and profile silhouette; preserve volumetric limbs and ear connections; inspect front, profiles, rear, top, bottom and three-quarter views; export geometry for Blender only after accepting shape. Do not label an approximation as identical.
