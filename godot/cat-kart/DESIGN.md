# Cat Kart — reference character edition

Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility Web game with pixel presentation. Cats, karts and streets have real geometry. The six approved front views are retained in art/references. Anatomical heads have depressed eye sockets, embedded volumetric eyeballs, curved iris artwork, eyelid rims, a solid nose, muzzle pads, mouth creases and fine whiskers. Each cat has independent forelegs, shoulders, forepaws, toes, thighs, hind paws and thick ear shells with recessed pink bowls. The white bib is fur colour on the curved ribcage. Side and rear surfaces are reconstructed because the references only specify a front view.

## Editing

Open project.godot in Godot, blender/source/anatomical_cats_turnaround.blend for front/side/back comparison, or blender/source/six_anatomical_cats.blend for the six identities. Each cat also has an editable _driver.blend and _kart.blend. Rear tabby markings are broken strokes rather than horizontal rings. blender/reference_driver.py builds the cranium base; blender/anatomical_driver.py replaces pictured anatomy with geometry. blender/rebuild.py builds the kart and exports six GLBs into models. Run Blender with --background --python godot/cat-kart/blender/rebuild.py. Append -- silverbelt munchkin to rebuild only named cats. models.gd imports the hierarchy and animates wheels, steering, driver lean, head and tail using articulated nodes, not a skinned skeleton. Keep imported texture PNGs in Web exports.

## Course and collisions

The 1,028m circuit has market streets, a fork, industrial streets and docks. The two joining fork routes are each approximately 247m long. Building placement checks footprints against both roads. Buildings and guardrails have StaticBody3D collision shapes. Barriers open at branch junctions. Swept CharacterBody3D player motion slides and loses speed after impact, preventing tunnelling even when one movement step crosses a thin wall. Lane limits are an additional backup.

Six selectable cats race five AI opponents over three laps. Features include drift boosts, boost/slow strips, jump flips, shield/pulse/boost items, minimap, ranking, recovery, pause, restart, finish, engine audio and a guided track tour. AI follows route samples and lane offsets. Player road lookup uses nearby spatial cells with a global recovery fallback. Engine audio uses a native looping PCM sample with speed-dependent pitch, avoiding per-sample GDScript calls during races.

## Validation and export

Hold S / Down / Brake-Reverse to brake, then reverse at up to 8m/s. W / Up brakes out of reverse before accelerating forward. Reverse steering and wheel rotation follow signed velocity. Road arrows and fork signs indicate the legal direction; the back of each sign says NO ENTRY. WRONG WAY appears after 0.35s of movement against the local road tangent on either route, including reverse travel. Pointing backward while stationary does not trigger it, and reversing in the legal travel direction clears it. Reverse travel cannot add lap progress.

Run Godot --headless --path godot/cat-kart --editor --import, then --script bake_world.gd -- --bake to generate compressed built scenes. --script tests/race_test.gd checks identities, animation, fork lengths, open junctions, high-speed and airborne wall sweeps, reverse movement and steering, wrong-way detection on both routes, AI, items, jumps, pause, checkpoint protection, finish and restart. Export with --export-pack Web followed by the absolute path to public/games/cat-kart/index.pck in pet-function. Host preview sync uses SNABBB_USE_LOCAL_PET=1 with scripts/prepare-pet.mjs.

Static scenery is merged by material into spatial chunks. Vertex colours retain the original paint colours without separate draws for every colour. The scenery now has approximately 200 render meshes instead of 1,627; the regression suite enforces a budget of 350. Mesh levels of detail are regenerated after batching for both scenery and karts. High detail uses a 0.6 resolution scale; Fast uses 0.5, cheaper soft contact shadows and a stronger distant-mesh simplification threshold. Menus retain their full resolution. Auto switches to Fast after two consecutive five-second windows below 38 FPS. The Detail button cycles Auto, High and Fast. Raised streets have masonry foundations outside the elevated fork. Browser FPS/draw measurements are diagnostics, not a guaranteed frame rate. Mobile performance and touch controls require separate device testing. Multiplayer and backend rewards are not implemented.
