# Art production — built-in imagegen

Saved project assets: `mechanical-atlas.png` (original transparent atlas, used via Godot AtlasTexture regions without painting or flattening its alpha) and `coral-coast.png` (scrolling background). Built-in image generation was used, not the API/CLI fallback. Style was informed by the existing richly illustrated games room and stadium.

## Mechanical atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE production sprite atlas for a premium 2D top-down airplane arcade game in a warm, richly shaded illustrated virtual-pet game world. Create one square transparent PNG sprite sheet with exactly FOUR separate sprites in a clean 2 by 2 grid, equal quadrants, large transparent gutters. Top left: heroic ivory and teal twin-engine fighter, warm brass trims, tiny tasteful paw insignia on wings, blue glass canopy, gun barrels, layered panel seams, rivets, propellers. Top right: copper-red enemy fighter with silver metal panels and dark glass canopy. Bottom left: large olive-gunmetal four-engine heavy bomber with beautifully engineered metallic wings, multiple engine nacelles, weapons and armor. Bottom right: steel battleship from directly overhead with distinct bow, deck planks, turrets, bridge, antennae. All exactly orthographic overhead, noses/bow pointing STRAIGHT UP, complete objects entirely contained in their own quadrant, centered, no perspective tilt. Polished hand-painted game sprite aesthetic, substantial bevel shading, crisp dark outlines, tiny mechanical highlights, appealing solid toy-like forms and genuine material detail. Lighting consistent from upper left. Not simplistic flat vector silhouettes, not photorealistic. NO shadows cast outside the objects, NO flame or particles, NO UI, NO text, NO background, NO ground. True alpha transparency. Square atlas.

## Coast prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape BACKGROUND TEXTURE ONLY for a polished illustrated top-down coastal airplane arcade shooter belonging to a cozy detailed virtual-pet game. A strict overhead view of a turquoise navy ocean with subtle textured flowing water, warm sunlit foam, small ornate tropical islands along the FAR LEFT and FAR RIGHT margins only, sandy rocky shores, tiny palms, richly hand-painted shaded stone and foliage. Broad unobstructed dark blue teal water occupies the central 80 percent for legible bullets and aircraft. Premium stylized 2D game environment art with fine material detail and warm cream golden lighting, crisp illustrated edges, matching a richly shaded cozy arcade-room and whimsical sports stadium world, NOT minimalist polygons and NOT photorealism. Wide landscape composition 3:2. No aircraft, no ships, no UI, no lettering, no logos, no horizon, no perspective sky, no characters. Must be usable as a slowly scrolling game background, upper and lower edges blend in similar open water.

## Runtime treatment

Aircraft have alpha-preserving region crops, moving propeller highlights, exhaust lighting, shadows and muzzle flashes. Friendly projectiles have cream cores and cyan tails; enemy bullets use orange-red glows. Shields use translucent hexagonal cells, rotating Fresnel arcs and hit pulses. Explosions use shock rings, sparks and fading smoke. Visual randomness is isolated from gameplay randomness; active particles are capped at 500. All effects stay in the clipped combat layer and obey existing pause/resize rules. UI uses brass trim, fasteners and beveled metal plates with a paw emblem.
