# Gulu player aircraft, levels 1–10

Mode: built-in imagegen. Final asset: `gulu-player-aircraft-levels-1-10-concept.png`.

This is the player aircraft concept board, not an enemy atlas. Order: top row 1–5, bottom row 6–10. Gulu faces the nose (upward); its rear head, ears, striped back and cream paws are enlarged so the pilot reads clearly.

1. Simple single-propeller trainer, small twin guns.
2. Reinforced wing tips and extra wing guns.
3. Twin propeller engines and four gun mounts.
4. Armored twin-engine craft with missile pods.
5. Swept-wing blue jet with multiple weapon mounts.
6. Teal energy fighter with cyan cannons.
7. Dark delta stealth craft with plasma rails.
8. Heavy blue/steel fighter with reactor module.
9. Gold/ivory advanced fighter with cyan reactor.
10. Ultimate ivory/gold/teal heavy energy fighter, layered wings and weapon batteries.

Status: visual design concept only; not yet loaded by the game. Adjacent aircraft silhouettes touch in the concept board, so equal-cell slicing is unsuitable for production sprite extraction. Existing game aircraft and enemies are unchanged.
