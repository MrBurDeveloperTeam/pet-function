# Hangar background

Mode: built-in imagegen. Final asset: public/games/air-strike/hangar.png.

Use case stylized-concept. Production background for a premium PIXEL ART airplane arcade game's hangar lobby. Wide landscape 16:9 composition. A huge industrial aircraft maintenance hangar viewed from ABOVE at a steep near-top-down angle so a top-down airplane sprite can be composited on the central platform. Architecture inspired by imposing mecha workshop: heavy steel wall beams, overhead crane rails, hanging chain hoists, teal service consoles, warm amber work lamps, glass roof skylights, pipes, grated catwalks and tool benches around the outer margins. Center is an EMPTY broad aircraft service cradle: rectangular floor inspection pit, four squat maintenance support posts and rails, hydraulic jacks and fork stands arranged around its perimeter, believable raised metal supporting structure ready to hold a plane. Keep the central 40% x 65% clear for the aircraft; no aircraft, cats, robots or people in image. Floors gunmetal/teal with cream/yellow safety stripes, discreet gold highlights matching cream-teal arcade aircraft. Deliberate detailed 16-bit pixel clusters, crisp edges, restricted material shades, no blur, dramatic skylight plus warm practical lighting. Upper and lower outer 12% relatively dark and quiet for native UI overlay. No words, numbers, logos, labels or interface panels. Opaque background. Emphasize a mechanical maintenance bay and support racks, not a flat runway.
